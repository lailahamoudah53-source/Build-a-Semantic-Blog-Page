Artificial Intelligent & Multiple Agents System

Imagine you're in a massive warehouse — hundreds of robots moving packages at the same time. How do they make sure they don't crash into each other and that everything runs on time? This is exactly what Multi-Agent Systems solve — and what I'll share with you today.

What is a multiple agents system (MAS)?

• MAS: A system of multiple interacting agents that collaborate or compete to solve problems.

• When multiple agents interact, they form a Multi-Agent System. Each agent has partial knowledge, but they coordinate to achieve system-wide goals.

• For example, autonomous drones surveying a disaster site or smart devices in a smart home.

Benefits of multiple agents system

  • Scalability: Distributed tasks across agents.
  • Flexibility: Agents can adapt, learn or be added/removed.
  • Robustness: No single point of failure.
